# Dashboard cache demand and freshness

The normal dashboard timer now refreshes components that an API caller requested during the preceding two minutes. It keeps its 30-second cadence. An idle backend performs no dashboard SQL, and a REST info read does not request recent or fastest lists or another workspace. Explicit all-workspace prewarm remains available; development fixtures still use that operation under their strict five-second caller deadline. Normal optional startup prewarm uses the requested-only path.

Info, recent checks and fastest proxies retain separate workspace/normalized-limit keys. Successful entries record completion time and expire after 30 seconds even when the checker generation stays unchanged. Zero, future and expired timestamps cannot hit. A cold or expired API read waits for a refresh; no expired snapshot is returned as a successful cache hit. Configuration generation and dirty-health checks still bracket loads, and local invalidation prevents an older flight from publishing after a committed settings change. REST and GraphQL now propagate their request contexts to these reads.

Requests and the timer share one refresh per key. A canceled follower leaves another caller's leader running. A live follower can retry once after another leader's cancellation. Bookkeeping never holds its mutex across SQL or a wait. Expiry pins active flights, then removes expired real demand at completion unless a reader renewed it. Internal refreshes do not renew demand. Explicit prewarm entries have a short storage grace without creating demand. Account deletion removes the deleted workspace's demand after commit. A recovered loader panic releases followers and permits a later refresh.

The six info-query workers and their SQL are unchanged. Existing delayed daily-history maintenance remains optional on SQL failure, but the refresh owns its children and waits for them. A fast cold info read can therefore take at least the existing two-second backfill delay. The context stays live on ordinary success, so successful maintenance is not canceled merely because the info SELECTs finished. Error/cancellation paths cancel and join their children. Explicit all-workspace prewarm groups those existing delays and joins them before returning, preserving the five-second fixture deadline without serially adding two seconds per workspace. Each owned child has a finite 30-second context.

Demand gating delays historical backfill work for an unread workspace. New statistics ingestion and retention/compaction policy are unchanged. The targeted retained-history test exercises a read-only database refusal, a later successful writable backfill, exact lifetime/week counts, preservation of raw history and a fresh same-generation reload after the age boundary. This does not establish production recovery or representative capacity.
